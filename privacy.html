Privacy Policy

Ruledrift · last updated 17 October 2026

Ruledrift is a small brain game built by one person. This policy describes exactly what the game does with your data - not what a template says a product like this usually does.

The short version. If you do not sign in, nothing you play ever leaves your device. If you do sign in, the only reason is to back up your own game history so it follows you to another device. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking of any kind.

If you never sign in

The game is fully playable with no account and with the network switched off. Your scores, streaks, XP, achievements and per-rule accuracy are kept in your own browser's local storage. They are not transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently, and we cannot recover them, because we never had them.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional and exists for one purpose: backing up your progress so it survives a cleared browser and follows you between devices. Sign-in is handled by Google or Discord. Once they confirm who you are, a small sign-in server at auth.mridulsaravanan.com signs you in to Google Firebase, which provides the accounts and database for this game.

What is stored when you are signed in:

  • Your email address and display name, as supplied by Google or Discord.
  • Your Ruledrift game profile: games played, scores, levels reached, streaks, XP, rank, achievements and per-rule accuracy.

What is never stored: your password (we never see it - Google and Discord handle that entirely), your contacts, your posts, your location, your device identifiers, and any information from your Google or Discord account beyond your email address and display name.

Your data sits in a single record that only your own signed-in account can read or write. This is enforced by the database's own security rules, not merely by application code.

Playing with friends: if you create or join a room, that room holds your display name, an anonymous account id, your marks on the shared grid and any clues you choose to share. Anyone signed in who has the room code can read it, so share codes only with people you are playing with. Rooms close after 24 hours and the host can delete one at any time.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics and no tracking pixels. The only outside script is Google's own sign-in button, and it loads only when you choose Google.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No email marketing. Signing in does not subscribe you to anything.

Google user data

Ruledrift requests only your basic profile and email address from Google, and uses them solely to identify your account and to attach your saved game history to it. Ruledrift's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That data is never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never read by a human.

Deleting your data

The game has a real delete button, not a request form:

  • On your device: Settings → Your data → Delete all. This wipes local storage immediately.
  • In the cloud: Settings → Your data → Delete account copy removes your backup entirely. You can also revoke Ruledrift's access at any time from your Google account permissions page or your Discord authorised apps page.

If you would rather it be done for you, email the address below and it will be deleted.

Children

Ruledrift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A privacy claim that quietly goes stale is worse than never having made one, so the game's own footer text is updated whenever the truth of it changes.
